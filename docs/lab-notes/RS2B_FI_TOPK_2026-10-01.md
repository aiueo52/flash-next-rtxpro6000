## 0. Why

RS2 (specs/RS2_SPARSE_RS_2026-10-01.md) draws each draft token from a 64-token support. Its proposal costs
22.2 us per draft step (fp32 logits, n=1; greedy's top-1 costs 3.6 us): `_draft_partial_topk_kernel` (9.7 us,
24 CTAs, int64 `tl.topk` of 64 out of 2048) and `_draft_finalize_kernel` (12.5 us, one CTA, int64 `tl.topk` of 64
out of 1536 candidates). A block/warps sweep saves only 1-2.7 us. The S1 traces show 9.8-15 draft steps per verify,
so the proposal adds 0.16-0.35 ms per verify (1.5-3% of a 10-13 ms step).

RS2b replaces the 2048-way partial and the 1536-candidate finalize with FlashInfer's radix top-k over the whole row
(the same `flashinfer.top_k` SV2 uses in the verify) and a finalize over its 64 entries. Target: <= 10 us per draft
step at n=1, fp32.

## 1. Exactness contract

- **NaN-free rows: bit-identical to RS2** (`topk_impl="triton"`): `probs`, `tokens`, `topk_p`, `topk_index`, the
  chain column and `positions`. RS2's order is its int64 key: value descending, then index ascending, with -0.0
  equal to 0.0 and NaN as -inf (`_logit_keys`).
- FlashInfer `top_k(..., deterministic=True, tie_break=1)` keeps the smaller index among equal values at the
  selection boundary, so its 64-entry SET equals RS2's on NaN-free rows; its output order does not matter, since
  the finalize rebuilds RS2's keys from (value, index) and sorts the 64 entries itself (`tl.topk` over 64).
- NaN rows: FlashInfer ranks NaN above +inf, so a row with m NaNs (m < 64) holds m NaN entries and the 64-m best
  real values. The finalize maps NaN to -inf (`_logit_keys`), so the NaN entries sort last with probability 0, and q
  stays a valid distribution (losslessness needs only that the same q both draws X and verifies). RS2b then differs
  from RS2 only in those last m ranks. All-NaN rows match RS2 (both become 64 -inf ties, smallest indices).
- -0.0 vs 0.0: FlashInfer orders -0.0 below 0.0; RS2 ties them. Only a 0.0 logit exactly at the 64th-place boundary
  can change the set. Accepted (measure-zero for real logits); tests avoid it.
