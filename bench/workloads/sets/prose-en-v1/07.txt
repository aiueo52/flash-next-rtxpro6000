Write an original literary story of 4500–5500 words, in English prose without headings, bullets or an afterword. The setting is a small railway plant nursery; the viewpoint character is a volunteer gardener. Introduce a wooden peg naturally in the first paragraph. During one ordinary shift, seedling labels fade in the order of unspoken farewells. Develop several patient scenes with concrete work, hesitant dialogue and an understated emotional change. Vary sentence length and imagery. Avoid a repeated refrain or an explanation of the phenomenon. In the final scene the opening object should carry a different meaning. Give the full story rather than an outline or summary.
