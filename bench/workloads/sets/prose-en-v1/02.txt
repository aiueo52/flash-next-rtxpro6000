Write an original literary story of 4500–5500 words, in English prose without headings, bullets or an afterword. The setting is a closed hillside tram depot; the viewpoint character is the last ticket clerk. Introduce a brass washer naturally in the first paragraph. During one ordinary shift, the destination blinds show rooms from her childhood. Develop several patient scenes with concrete work, hesitant dialogue and an understated emotional change. Vary sentence length and imagery. Avoid a repeated refrain or an explanation of the phenomenon. In the final scene the opening object should carry a different meaning. Give the full story rather than an outline or summary.
