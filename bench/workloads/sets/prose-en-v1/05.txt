Write an original literary story of 4500–5500 words, in English prose without headings, bullets or an afterword. The setting is a disused mountain cable station; the viewpoint character is a rope inspector. Introduce a wool glove naturally in the first paragraph. During one ordinary shift, cargo labels arrive describing things still upstairs. Develop several patient scenes with concrete work, hesitant dialogue and an understated emotional change. Vary sentence length and imagery. Avoid a repeated refrain or an explanation of the phenomenon. In the final scene the opening object should carry a different meaning. Give the full story rather than an outline or summary.
