Write an original literary story of 4500–5500 words, in English prose without headings, bullets or an afterword. The setting is a municipal swimming pool in winter; the viewpoint character is a tile restorer. Introduce a folded towel naturally in the first paragraph. During one ordinary shift, dry lanes retain the sounds of absent swimmers. Develop several patient scenes with concrete work, hesitant dialogue and an understated emotional change. Vary sentence length and imagery. Avoid a repeated refrain or an explanation of the phenomenon. In the final scene the opening object should carry a different meaning. Give the full story rather than an outline or summary.
